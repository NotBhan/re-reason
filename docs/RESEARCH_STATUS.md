## 1. Current validated state

### Architecture and scope

* **Active architecture:** `NonlinearCandidateMGUModel` — 226,358 parameters, 212-dim state, 256-dim observation embedding, 146-dim candidate hidden, convex-bounded commit `S_t = (1−f_t)⊙S_{t−1} + f_t⊙C_t` with `C_t = tanh(MLP_cand([f_t⊙S_{t−1}, e_t]))`. Classified as a **known recurrence** (Update-Gate RNN / MGU family; Collins et al. 2017; Zhou et al. 2016) with an MLP parameterization variant — **no novelty claim** (`docs/reports/model_e_novelty_and_mechanism_audit.md`; `docs/reports/parameterization_isolation_audit.md`).
* **Scope guardrails:** Phase-2 machinery (branching, verifier, transactional commit, recursion, adaptive halting) is **not implemented and not currently pursued** (`docs/reports/verifier_feasibility.md` decision; `docs/06_risks_research_questions_and_future.md` deferred list). The target conceptual pipeline in `docs/02_core_architecture.md` remains a roadmap, not the active system.

### Official Phase-1 training objective

**Candidate/local supervision (Model E)** — adopted per the decision rule in `docs/reports/combined_objective_confirmation.md`:

```
L_E = CE(y_T)  +  (1/|W|) · Σ_t Σ_{r ∈ W_t} CE( head(C_t)_r , y_{t,r} )      (β = 1)
```

where `W_t` = registers written at step t, `head` = the existing decision head, `C_t` = the existing candidate vectors. The combined objective F (terminal + stepwise + candidate) was **not** adopted (F ≈ E on the primary metric; F does not close the candidate→commit gap), though F's ghost-elimination capability is documented in the F report.

### Frozen components and checkpoints

* Frozen: recurrent core, observation encoder, gate network, candidate MLP, convex commit, terminal readout, optimizer protocol, and the E/F checkpoints.
* Official checkpoints: `experiments/checkpoints/E_candidate_seed{42..46}_3000steps.pt` (official objective) and `experiments/checkpoints/F_combined_seed{42..46}_3000steps.pt` (control). SHA-256 values are recorded in `experiments/experiment_g_transitions_meta.json` and `experiments/experiment_h_interp_meta.json`; the H runner re-verifies hash stability before/after measurement.

### Key mechanism findings (each cited; interpret each at the level it was measured)

* **Terminal-only supervision was a first-order bottleneck.** Changing only the objective (same frozen architecture) lifted T=10 EM from 0.540 to 0.755 (`docs/reports/generator_repair_feasibility.md`).
* **State representation** is entangled but linearly decodable (~78% per-register at t=10); the gate carries op/destination information at 100% linear decodability; the candidate behaves as a **delta patch** on written registers (`docs/reports/mechanism_qa_diagnostic.md`, `docs/reports/mechanism_audit_round2.md`).
* **The dominant unsolved arithmetic failure is modular wrap-around** and, secondarily, repeated ADD chains (`docs/reports/mechanism_audit_round2.md`; `docs/reports/generator_repair_feasibility.md`).
* **The ADD candidate→commit residual** affects ~5.5% of candidate-correct ADD transitions, concentrated almost entirely in general two-register adds; the evidence is mixed between gate dilution and blend/readout corruption (`docs/reports/experiment_g_add_commit_gap.md`).
* **Scalar gate summaries are descriptive proxies only.** The element-wise vector-gate replay reproduces the commit bit-exactly, while its own mean-gate scalarization collapses decode accuracy (0.855/0.853 → 0.182/0.042 on candidate-correct general adds). Any commit-side reasoning must be coordinate-wise (`docs/reports/experiment_h_lambda_interpolation.md`).
* **The ADD candidate→commit residual is coordinate-selective and mixed.** Measured against the strongest competing class in decision space (exact, since the readout is a single linear layer), failures deliver ~78–80% of the candidate's decision-margin movement vs ~90% on successes (seed-paired dz ≈ −6, 5/5 seeds) and preferentially under-deliver the *positive* (decision-helping) coordinates while slightly over-delivering negative ones; failures also start from about half the candidate margin. At the same time, 44–48% of failures delivered at least the exact scalar-equivalent transfer needed (`ρ_margin ≥ α*_full`) yet landed off the scalar path in a different competitor's basin. Classification **C (both)** — under-movement dominant, placement/readout precision additionally required (`docs/reports/experiment_i_coordinate_commit.md`).
* **The commit/gate is a real but sub-dominant local bottleneck (interventional test).** A commit-only intervention on the full 212-dim gate (`f' = clip(γ f, 0, 1)`, recursive rollout, frozen checkpoints) monotonically raises decision-margin delivery (ρ_margin 0.884→0.930) and nets a small candidate→commit rescue only in a narrow window (net +5 transitions/seed at γ=1.10), but **no γ>1 improves T=10/T=20 EM and γ≥1.25 significantly degrades it**, breaking more baseline-correct sequences than it rescues and worsening wrap/repeated-ADD and SET/SWAP. γ<1 is strongly harmful, and a mean-matched coordinate-scrambled gate collapses EM to ≈0.01 (coordinate identity is essential). Classification **B (partial causal support)** — interventional with respect to the commit rule only; not a fix (`docs/reports/experiment_j_commit_intervention.md`).
* **The gate's *shape/balance* is a second, distinct local causal handle — and it also does not reach sequence capability (interventional test).** A coordinate-preserving monotone remap of the full gate about 0.5 (`f' = sigmoid(κ·logit(clip(f, 1e-6, 1−1e-6)))`, κ=2, recursive rollout, frozen checkpoints) changes gate *shape*, not magnitude (Δmean(f) −0.0035, Δmedian −0.0235, Δentropy −0.0827; mean transfer does **not** rise), and achieves the favorable delivery profile Experiment I identified: it raises decision-helping delivery (gw_pos 0.690→0.698, ρ_margin 0.884→0.902, Δm_commit +0.37, 5/5) **and lowers harmful delivery** (gw_neg 0.270→0.264, 5/5) — unlike uniform amplification, which raised `gw_neg` monotonically. Yet **no sequence endpoint improves** (E T=10 0.7547→0.7528, T=20 0.4068→0.4122, both n.s.), net transition recovery is negative (−7.6/seed), primary committed accuracy *falls* slightly (0.855→0.847), and the off-path failures survive (≈6% rescued; 100% competitor change). No significant operation-level regression; the controlled wrap builder improves (+6.75 pp). Classification **B (locally beneficial, sequence-level neutral)**; the commit rule is **frozen** (`docs/reports/experiment_k_gate_balance.md`).
* **The off-path candidate→commit residual is latent-state *placement*, not readout bias/calibration (measurement-only localization).** On the exact off-path population (n = 323 E / 353 F) the committed state **clears the strongest competitor that was binding at `S_prev` in 100% of cases** (`m(S_commit; a_prev)` median +1.96) yet the committed argmax is a **different** competitor and decodes to a **third value** in 100% of cases. The frozen linear readout is not mis-scoring an adequately placed state: removing the fixed bias (analysis only) flips only **1.2% (E) / 3.1% (F)** to correct and the bias accounts for a median 7.1% of the winning-pair margin. `off-path distance` alone does **not** distinguish off-path failures from successes (6.57 vs 6.36); perturbations around `S_commit` rarely reach the correct basin (perturbed-correct 1.7 / 7.9 / 13.1% at ε = 0.01/0.05/0.10). Category fractions (E / F): boundary-shortfall 0.0% / 0.0%, readout-calibration 1.2% / 3.1%, latent-misplacement 98.8% / 96.9%, mixed 0% / 0%. E and F are statistically indistinguishable on every signature. Classification **B — primarily latent-state placement** (`docs/reports/experiment_l_readout_localization.md`).
* **The third basin is a *systematic modular (parity)* region, and the wrap/carry hypothesis is rejected (measurement-only localization).** On the off-path population the wrong destination value **preserves the target's parity** in **73.7% (E) / 64.0% (F)** vs a **50% permutation null (p ≈ 0)** — an even modular offset (±2, ±4 dominate; |offset| ∈ {2,4} = 73.7% / 64.0%), **not** ±1 (only 9.9% / 11.0%). Wrap status does **not** change the wrong-value identity (TV 0.240 / 0.212 < 0.30 bar; mean `d_mod` to target 3.09 vs 3.19 for wrap vs no-wrap, E). The off-axis committed displacement is **directionally consistent within each model** (seed-level R = 0.256 ± 0.013 (E) / 0.306 ± 0.017 (F), all 5 seeds p ≈ 0 vs a within-seed sign-flip null; pooled R only 0.110 / 0.138 because checkpoints have different readouts) and mildly arithmetic-localized; the wrong *register* is **not** localized (flat 40-way winner == dest ≈ 0%); the third basin is **deep** (normalized true-vs-flat-winner distance −2.27 / −2.23 vs −0.90 / −0.84 for successes). Classification **M-A — systematic modular geometry** (E and F; `docs/reports/experiment_m_representation_geometry.md`).
* **The same-parity signature is *state-alignment-dominant*, not readout-dominant (measurement-only localization).** The frozen readout **does** contain a small but reproducible within-register parity organization in its value-weight geometry (same-parity cosine −0.042 vs different-parity −0.152; effect **+0.110 (E) / +0.111 (F)**, significant in **4/5** seeds, p ≈ 0.006–0.011; cross-register effect only +0.024; no bias parity structure). But it produces almost no **state-independent** same-parity preference: the pairing-break (readout-only / state-permutation) null is **0.521 / 0.504** vs chance 0.50, while the observed rate is **0.737 / 0.640** — the readout's marginal contribution is **8.9% / 2.5%** of the excess and the **state↔label alignment contributes 91.1% / 97.5%**. The parity-axis displacement magnitude is not off-path-specific (off 0.198 / 0.145 vs success 0.214 / 0.161), but the signed displacement tracks the target parity (corr −0.49 / −0.46). The readout value-label permutation is **degenerate** for parity (mapped-back = exact no-op; unmapped ≈ 0.50) and is reported as a necessary-condition control only. Classification **N-B — state-dominant parity alignment** (E and F; `docs/reports/experiment_n_parity_origin_localization.md`).
* **Parity alignment is a correlated diagnostic, not a demonstrated causal mechanism (interventional test).** Removing the W-derived parity component of the candidate — `C' = C − κ<C−S_prev, p_dest> p_dest`, through the **original frozen gate/commit** — rescues only a small, **non-specific** minority of off-path transitions: net **+8.0/seed (E) / +8.8 (F)** at κ=1, but a **norm-matched orthogonal control** rescues essentially the same (**+8.6 / +8.0**; only 2/5 seeds exceed the control). The parity interventions break **24.8% (E) / 22.2% (F)** of baseline-correct successes (vs 7.8% / 6.6% for the matched control) and **reduce sequence EM** (E T=10 0.7547→0.6505, −10.4 pp, 5/5 seeds, p=0.005; T=20 0.4068→0.3224; SET/SWAP/ADD-no-wrap/ADD-wrap all degrade). The commit-side stage control is likewise non-specific. Classification **O-D — no specific parity causality** (E and F; `docs/reports/experiment_o_representation_intervention.md`).
* **The sequence-level first-error budget is dominated by candidate-generation errors (Class A)** (measurement-only attribution). Of the sequences that fail at T=10, the earliest observed task-level error is a **candidate written-register error** in **59.7% (E) / 59.4% (F)** of failures, a **candidate→commit residual** in **32.5% / 31.8%**, and a **terminal/readout residual** in only **8.0% / 8.7%** (C3 near-boundary 5.8% / 5.6%, C2 2.0% / 3.1%, C1 0%, D 0%). Class A dominates in **5/5 seeds** (gap +0.27); at T=20 the budget is **A 67.2% / 67.6%, B 30.2% / 29.9%, C 2.6% / 2.5%** (A dominant 5/5, gap +0.37). Candidate-origin first errors are almost entirely **ADD** (no-wrap 568 + wrap 542 of ~1,203; SWAP 93; SET 0); commit-residual errors are even more wrap-weighted (377 wrap vs 274 no-wrap). **Untouched-register terminal corruption is 0**; written-register errors recover later in 74–80% but full-state correctness in only 31–42%; T=10 failure predicts T=20 failure (P=0.731 vs 0.544, OR ≈2.3). E and F are statistically indistinguishable. This is an **observed** budget, **not** a causal claim (`docs/reports/experiment_p_sequence_error_budget.md`).
* **The dominant candidate-generation (Class-A) failure is only weakly recoverable from the frozen candidate vector (measurement-only probes).** On the test ADD transitions where the frozen candidate head is wrong (Class-A, ~361/seed), the frozen head is 0% by construction, an analysis-only **linear** probe (fit on the disjoint TRAIN split, λ tuned on VAL, once on TEST) recovers the true written value **26.7% (E) / 25.3% (F)**, and a small fixed-capacity **nonlinear** probe **23.3% / 26.1%** — **no material nonlinear gain** (Δ = −0.035 / +0.008). Wrap ≈ no-wrap (0.273/0.263 E; 0.271/0.238 F); repeated ADD slightly weaker (0.222 vs isolated 0.301, E); the **raw sum is not easier than the modular target** (0.260 vs 0.267, E). The same probe reaches 93.4% on the candidate-correct control. Classification **Q-C — weak recoverability** (E primary; F agrees), and **R** shows it survives controlled capacity/distribution changes (`docs/reports/experiment_q_candidate_origin.md`, `docs/reports/experiment_r_candidate_representation.md`).
* **The weak recoverability is robust to bounded capacity and to size-matched hard-tail training; the only regime that raises apparent accuracy is memorization (R-D).** In a 2-D capacity {linear, MLP-16/32/64/128} × distribution {ordinary, size-matched ordinary, designated hard-tail (144/seed), augmented i.i.d. hard pool (~1,500/seed)} test on the same untouched canonical-TEST hard tail: ordinary-trained hard-test accuracy is **flat 0.244 → 0.236** across capacity (train → 0.99); the designated hard-tail gives only **+0.068** over the size-matched control (5/5, < 0.15 bar); the augmented pool reaches **0.480** (Δ vs ordinary +0.225) but **collapses to 0.176 on held-out operand combinations vs 0.493 on seen pairs** (gap 0.317, chance 0.10) while training accuracy climbs to 0.97. Raw-sum not easier than modular; candidate-correct decodability degrades for hard-specialized probes; E and F agree. Classification **R-D — memorization / transfer artifact**: the apparent gains are operand-combination memorization, not recoverability, which **strengthens the information-limit interpretation** and keeps no candidate-side intervention licensed (`docs/reports/experiment_r_candidate_representation.md`).
* **The candidate-side loss is not output-localised: no internal candidate activation is more recoverable than `C_t` (measurement-only internal-activation probes; S-C).** Probing the frozen `NonlinearCandidateMGU` candidate network's **actual internal activations** — `S_prev`(212), `f_gate`(212), `h_reset`(212), `cand_input`(468), `h1_pre`(146), `h1_post`(146), `out_pre`(212), `C_t`(212); the candidate MLP has **one** hidden layer, not two — under R's capacity × distribution design on the same untouched canonical-TEST Class-A tail: the hard-trained (D1x/mlp32) chain **weakly increases toward the output** (`cand_input` 0.246 → `h1_pre` 0.378 → `h1_post` 0.427 → `out_pre` 0.432 → **`C_t` 0.463**), so `C_t` is the most (weakly) recoverable stage, not the weakest; **no internal stage materially beats `C_t`** (best internal `out_pre` gain **−0.031**, 0/5 seeds positive). Under ordinary training (D0) the chain is flat-to-weak (0.128 → 0.217 → 0.215 → 0.220 → 0.238). **Held-out-operand generalization fails at every stage** (D1x held-out 0.045–0.176, all ≤ 0.25; seen 0.29–0.49; gaps 0.24–0.35), so the internal "gains" are the same memorization R found for `C_t`. No nonlinear boundary drops recoverability (GELU +0.049, tanh +0.031; postactivations slightly *more* decodable); the candidate input is itself weak (`cand_input` D0 0.128 / D1x 0.246); wrap ≈ no-wrap everywhere; raw-sum not consistently easier than modular; Class-A value geometry stays weakly separable and rotates away from the candidate-correct mean toward the output. `C_t` reproduces R exactly (0.244/0.238/0.463); E and F agree. Classification **S-C — computation-limited**: consistent with the limitation being **before or throughout** the candidate computation, **not** an output-projection/geometry limit; observational, no intervention licensed (`docs/reports/experiment_s_candidate_internal.md`).
* **The Class-A information is weak throughout the frozen upstream pipeline, and the observation encoding is structurally operand-blind (measurement-only upstream-representation probes; T-D).** Probing the real frozen pipeline's **upstream** stages (`raw_obs`, `enc_h1_pre`, `enc_h1_post`, `enc_out_pre`, `e_t`, `S_prev`, `gate_pre`, `f_gate`, `h_reset`, `cand_input`, `C_t`; captured read-only via forward hooks) under R/S's capacity × distribution design on the same untouched canonical-TEST Class-A tail: the **encoder output `e_t` decodes the destination at 1.000 but `operand_a`/`operand_b` at 0.110/0.136** (chance 0.10) — the ADD observation carries no operand values, so the target information must come from the state pathway; the **state path is only descriptively stronger** (`S_prev` 0.167 → `gate_pre` 0.191 → `f_gate` 0.213 → `h_reset` **0.239**, vs encoder-path best `enc_h1_pre` 0.141; difference **+0.098**, below the 0.10 bar) and **does not generalise** (seen→held-out collapses: `h_reset` 0.261→0.046, `cand_input` 0.286→0.045, `C_t` 0.493→0.176); the sigmoid boundary does **not** lower accessibility (`gate_pre`→`f_gate` +0.022 in-distribution, −0.013 held-out); `cand_input` (0.246) ≈ `h_reset` alone; **no stage reaches the 0.50 bar** (max `C_t` 0.463) or 0.25 held-out (max 0.176); raw-sum not easier than modular, wrap ≈ no-wrap; E and F agree. `C_t` reproduces R/S exactly (0.244/0.238/0.463). Classification **T-D — weak throughout**: the limitation is already present before/across the tested upstream representations; observational, no intervention licensed (`docs/reports/experiment_t_upstream_representation.md`).
* **The raw task interface *does* determine the operands/target, but bounded flat probes do not access it (measurement-only raw-observation/history analysis; U-D).** Two independent evidence families on the raw 21-dim one-hot observation history (state-free), on the canonical TEST: **exact interface evidence** and **learned probes**. The **current observation is operand-blind and target-ambiguous** (L=1: destination Bayes **1.000**, `operand_a`/`operand_b` **0.437/0.444**, written/modular target **0.290**; every L=1 history ambiguous; 16 unique histories over 15,763 rows). The **interface-identifiable history horizon is L\*=4** (target Bayes **0.290 → 0.402 → 0.998** at L1→L2→L4; operands 0.437/0.444 → 0.999/0.999; collision rate 1.000 → 0.016; ambiguous mass 1.000 → 0.004), and the **full causal prefix is uniquely sufficient** (target/operands/raw-sum Bayes **1.000**, zero ambiguity). But the **bounded probe family stays weak**: best full-prefix all-ADD probe **0.218** (MLP-128; MLP-32 0.205, linear 0.164), Class-A ~0.09; held-out operand accuracy ≤ 0.25 at every length (full-prefix 0.121); the full-prefix probe memorizes train windows (train acc 0.913) but not the rule; **Bayes−probe gap 0.782**; operand probes rise only 0.077→0.290 despite an operand ceiling of 1.0 (so the limitation is not the modular composition). Exact prefix replay reproduces the ground truth 100%; wrap/no-wrap/repeated/isolated all reach Bayes ≈1.0 by L=4 (spread ≤0.096); raw-sum not easier than modular; **E and F are identical** (the raw interface and all-ADD population are model-independent). Classification **U-D — interface-identifiable but probe-limited**: the information is present at the raw task interface, but the tested bounded flat-probe family does not exploit it; observational, no intervention licensed (`docs/reports/experiment_u_task_interface.md`).
* **Providing explicit temporal structure does not resolve the U-D gap: bounded causal sequence readers are also weak (measurement-only sequence-aware access test; V-B).** Two predeclared, parameter-bounded causal readers on the raw 22-feature (21-dim observation + validity bit) per-timestep stream — a single-layer causal **GRU** (hidden 85, 29,429 params) and a **causal dilated 1-D convolution** (TCN-style, channels 52, 28,983 params), each within the exact U MLP-128 budget (29,578) — under U's history grid/splits/populations with held-out-operand control: Class-A accuracy (E, 5-seed mean) is **0.083 (GRU) / 0.112 (TCN)** at L=4 and **0.080 / 0.109** at the full prefix, versus U's flat MLP-128 **0.089 / 0.086** (gain **+0.023**, sub-material); all-ADD: L4 0.115/0.176/0.198, full 0.147/0.214/**0.218** — the sequence readers never beat the flat baseline materially. No reader reaches 0.50; held-out is ≤0.25 everywhere (0.105–0.212); the exact Class-A Bayes ceiling at the same horizon is **0.999 (L4) / 1.000 (full)** → **Bayes−reader gap 0.887**. The readers do learn *some* structure (GRU: `op_dest` 1.000, operands 0.248/0.207 at the full prefix) but not the target; per-seed all-ADD full-prefix accuracy is highly variable (TCN 0.093–0.306), indicating memorisation. Causality is verified (randomised-future invariance; leaky builder rejected; bidirectional GRU rejected; causal TCN verified); E and F agree (all-ADD identical; Class-A E−F ≤0.016). Classification **V-B — sequence-limited**: the U-D gap persists for the tested bounded causal sequence readers, and weak accessibility is not resolved merely by providing temporal structure; observational, no intervention licensed (`docs/reports/experiment_v_sequence_access.md`).
* **Increasing unique training data does not rescue the weak accessibility: the bounded readers are data-saturated over 1500–10000 (measurement-only training-data-scale test; W-B).** U's flat MLP-128 anchor and V's causal GRU/TCN, each within the U MLP-128 budget, are fit on **fresh, pairwise sequence-disjoint i.i.d. ordinary-ADD pools** of 1500 / 4000 / 10000 transitions (the *reduced* predeclared ladder; the full ladder is 1500/8000/32000), with exactly **200 full-batch updates at every scale**, and the canonical VAL/TEST/reps/populations unchanged. Class-A accuracy (E, 5-seed mean) is flat: MLP-128 L4 **0.089→0.076→0.070** (Lfull 0.101→0.079→0.071; the flat anchor *declines* with data), GRU L4 **0.083→0.102→0.116**, TCN L4 **0.106→0.107→0.114**; best top-scale reader **0.116** (GRU L4), best per-reader gain **+0.033** (sub-material); no reader ≥0.50 and held-out ≤0.206 everywhere (bar 0.30); the exact Class-A Bayes ceiling at L4 is 0.999 → **Bayes−reader gap 0.883** (best-reader gap barely moves: 0.892 at 1500). The readers do **not** memorize — L4 training accuracy is 0.13–0.23 and the MLP-128 anchor's training accuracy *falls* 0.375→0.232 as the pool grows (underfitting under a fixed-capacity/fixed-budget protocol). E and F agree (both W-B). Classification **W-B — data-saturated/bounded-limited**: over this range the V-B weak accessibility is **not** a simple training-data limitation under the fixed bounded reader/optimization protocol, and is consistent with a bounded-capacity/algorithmic limit; the fixed-budget vs bounded-capacity split is left unresolved; observational, no causal claim, no intervention licensed (`docs/reports/experiment_w_data_scale.md`).

## 2. Historical progression (how the current state was reached)

1. **Phase 1 diagnostics (2026-10-04):** terminal-supervision failure classified as memorization + credit-assignment, not representation failure → investigate gating.
2. **Phase 1.5 causal ablation:** LayerNorm ruled out as primary cause; unbounded gated variant insufficient; bounded convex interpolation identified as the untested hypothesis.
3. **Phase 1.5 bounded convex ablation:** Model E (bounded convex) adopted — 45.55% vs 11.25% (GRU) / 6.13% (baseline).
4. **Novelty & mechanism audit:** recurrence classified as known (UGRNN/MGU family); convexity + boundedness as causal drivers; no novelty claim.
5. **Parameterization isolation:** nonlinear state–observation mixing decisive; MGU pre-gating valuable; **Nonlinear Candidate MGU** (54.28%) locked as the core; FLOP accounting corrected (amendment precedent).
6. **Mechanism Q&A + Round 2 audits:** state geometry, gate function, delta-patch candidate; bottleneck localized upstream (wrap-around); decision FREEZE AND ADVANCE toward branching.
7. **Branching feasibility (2026-10-05):** oracle K=8 EM 0.6913, but per-draw information at chance; matched noise recovers ~46% of the gain → branching hypothesis not validated; oracle not achievable.
8. **Learned-verifier feasibility:** headline AUROC is an artifact (always-deterministic top-1 ≈ selector top-1); no useful decision-relevant signal → verifier/branching abandoned for now.
9. **Generator-repair feasibility (supervision objectives):** terminal supervision identified as the bottleneck; candidate/local (E) strongest (0.755 / 0.407).
10. **Combined-objective confirmation (Model F):** F ≈ E on the primary metric, no gap closure → **candidate/local adopted as the official objective**; objective sweeps stopped.
11. **Experiment G:** ADD candidate→commit gap localized; gate-dilution vs blend/readout evidence mixed (classification C); scalar mean-gate flagged as a proxy.
12. **Experiment H:** scalar interpolation path decodable only near the candidate end; vector-gate structure decisive; commit misses the decodable band in scalar projection; next measurement specified at the coordinate level.
13. **Experiment I (2026-10-06):** decision-space (strongest-competitor margin) and exact coordinate decomposition of the element-wise commit; under-movement present and seed-consistent, but ~45% of failures delivered sufficient scalar-equivalent transfer and still failed off-path → classification **C (both)**; E and F indistinguishable on the residual.
14. **Experiment J (2026-10-06):** pre-registered commit-only intervention on the full 212-dim gate; margin delivery rises monotonically with γ and a narrow net transition rescue exists, but no γ>1 improves T10/T20 EM and γ≥1.25 harms it; mean-matched coordinate scrambling collapses EM → classification **B (partial causal support)**; the commit/gate is a real but sub-dominant local bottleneck and coordinate identity is essential.
15. **Experiment K (2026-10-07):** pre-registered two-condition *shape/balance* intervention (`f' = sigmoid(κ·logit(f))`, κ=2 vs κ=1) on the full gate; the remap lowers mean/median/entropy (not amplification) and achieves the favorable delivery profile (gw_pos↑, gw_neg↓, ρ_margin↑, shortfall↓, 5/5) yet no T10/T20 EM improvement, negative net transition recovery, and surviving off-path failures → classification **B (locally beneficial, sequence-level neutral)**; commit-rule modification (magnitude and shape) is frozen.
16. **Experiment L (2026-10-07):** measurement-only, frozen-readout decomposition of the off-path population; the committed state clears the intended competitor (100%) but lands on a different competitor (100%, third value); removing the fixed bias flips only 1–3% to correct; `off-path distance` does not distinguish failures → classification **B (primarily latent-state placement)**; the readout-bias explanation is measured away and the next measurement moves to representation geometry.
17. **Experiment M (2026-10-07):** measurement-only geometry of the third basin; the wrong value preserves the target's **parity** (74% / 64% vs 50% null, p ≈ 0; even modular offsets ±2/±4, not ±1); wrap status does not change the wrong-value identity (wrap/carry hypothesis rejected); the off-axis direction is seed-level consistent (R ≈ 0.26 / 0.31) but weak pooled; the wrong register is not localized → classification **M-A (systematic modular geometry, parity not wrap)**; the parity structure's origin (state vs frozen readout) remains unresolved.
18. **Experiment N (2026-10-07):** measurement-only parity-origin localization; the frozen readout has a small reproducible within-register parity geometry (effect +0.11, 4/5 seeds) but almost no state-independent same-parity preference (pairing-break null 0.52/0.50); the observed 0.74/0.64 is 91%/98% state↔label alignment → classification **N-B (state-dominant parity alignment)**; the readout value-label permutation is degenerate and the readout-side fix is not indicated.
19. **Experiment O (2026-10-07):** interventional, weight-frozen representation-side test removing the W-derived parity component of the candidate through the original frozen gate/commit; a small off-path rescue (+8/seed) is **not specific** (matched orthogonal control +8.6/seed), breaks 24.8% of baseline successes, and reduces sequence EM (T=10 −10.4 pp) → classification **O-D (no specific parity causality)**; parity is a correlated diagnostic, and representation-side parity intervention is ruled out.
20. **Experiment P (2026-10-07):** measurement-only sequence-level first-error attribution; the earliest observed task-level error is a candidate-generation error in **59.7% (E) / 67.2% (T=20)** of T=10/T=20 failures, a candidate→commit residual in 32.5% / 30.2%, and a terminal/readout residual in 8.0% / 2.6% (near-boundary); untouched-register corruption 0; candidate/commit written errors recover 74–80% but full-state only 31–42% → the observed budget is **candidate-generation-dominated** (5/5 seeds), robust across E/F, and is an **observed** budget, not a causal claim.
21. **Experiment Q (2026-10-07):** measurement-only candidate-generation recoverability; on the Class-A population (test ADD where the frozen candidate head is wrong) an analysis-only linear probe recovers the true written value only **26.7% (E) / 25.3% (F)** and a small nonlinear probe **23.3% / 26.1%** (no gain), versus 0% frozen and 10% chance; wrap ≈ no-wrap, repeated slightly weaker, raw sum not easier than modular → classification **Q-C (weak recoverability)**; the strongest evidence so far for a candidate-generation **representation** limitation, but not causal and not a licensed intervention.
22. **Experiment R (2026-10-07):** measurement-only capacity × distribution recoverability test on the same hard tail; ordinary-trained accuracy is **flat across capacity** (0.244→0.236), the size-matched designated hard-tail gives only **+0.068**, and the augmented i.i.d. hard pool raises apparent accuracy to **0.480** but **collapses to 0.176 on held-out operand combinations vs 0.493 seen** (gap 0.317) while training accuracy reaches 0.97 → classification **R-D (memorization / transfer artifact)**; the weak recoverability is robust, the information-limit interpretation is strengthened, and no candidate-side intervention is licensed.

23. **Experiment S (2026-10-07):** measurement-only **candidate-INTERNAL** activation probes (8 predeclared stages captured read-only via forward hooks from the real `NonlinearCandidateMGU` candidate network — one hidden layer) under R's capacity × distribution design with held-out-operand controls on the same hard tail; the D1x chain **weakly increases toward the output** (`cand_input` 0.246 → `C_t` 0.463), no internal stage beats `C_t` (best internal gain −0.031, 0/5 seeds), held-out-operand generalization fails at every stage (0.045–0.176), no nonlinear boundary drops recoverability, and the candidate input is itself weak → classification **S-C (computation-limited)**; the loss is **not** output-localised and the question moves upstream (`docs/reports/experiment_s_candidate_internal.md`).

24. **Experiment T (2026-10-08):** measurement-only **upstream-representation** probes (11 predeclared stages captured read-only via forward hooks on the real encoder/gate/candidate submodules) under R/S's design with held-out-operand controls on the same hard tail; the encoder output `e_t` is **structurally operand-blind** (destination 1.000, operands at chance 0.110/0.136), the state path is only **descriptively** stronger (`h_reset` 0.239 vs encoder best 0.141 = +0.098, below the 0.10 bar) and **collapses on held-out operands** (0.261→0.046), the sigmoid does not lower accessibility, `cand_input` ≈ `h_reset` alone, and no stage reaches the 0.50 bar (max `C_t` 0.463) or 0.25 held-out → classification **T-D (weak throughout)**; the limitation is already present before/across the tested upstream representations (`docs/reports/experiment_t_upstream_representation.md`).

25. **Experiment U (2026-10-08):** measurement-only **raw-observation / task-encoding** localisation on the state-free causal history `x_0..x_t` (exact collision/Bayes evidence + R/S/T learned probes) on the canonical TEST; the **current observation is operand-blind and target-ambiguous** (L=1 target Bayes 0.290; operands 0.437/0.444; every history ambiguous), the **interface-identifiable history horizon is L\*=4** (target 0.998, operands 0.999), the **full causal prefix is uniquely sufficient** (Bayes 1.000), yet the **bounded flat probes stay weak** (best full-prefix 0.218 all-ADD, ~0.09 Class-A; held-out ≤0.25; Bayes−probe gap 0.782) → classification **U-D (interface-identifiable but probe-limited)**; the raw task interface contains the target information (so T's operand-blindness is a property of the single-step observation/encoder, not of the task input), but the tested bounded reader does not exploit it (`docs/reports/experiment_u_task_interface.md`).

26. **Experiment V (2026-10-08):** measurement-only **bounded sequence-aware access test** with two predeclared parameter-bounded causal readers (single-layer causal **GRU** hidden 85 / 29,429 params; **causal dilated 1-D convolution** channels 52 / 28,983 params), each within the exact U MLP-128 budget (29,578), under U's history grid/splits/populations with held-out-operand control; Class-A accuracy (E) **0.083 (GRU) / 0.112 (TCN)** at L=4 and **0.080 / 0.109** at the full prefix vs the flat U MLP-128 **0.089 / 0.086**, all-ADD ≤ the flat baseline, no reader ≥0.50, held-out ≤0.25 everywhere, **Bayes−reader gap 0.887** → classification **V-B (sequence-limited)**: the U-D gap persists for the tested bounded causal sequence readers, so weak accessibility is not resolved merely by providing temporal structure; the readers recover `op_dest` (GRU 1.000) and operands partially (0.21–0.25) but not the target, indicating memorisation rather than rule learning (`docs/reports/experiment_v_sequence_access.md`).
27. **Experiment W (2026-10-09):** measurement-only **bounded-reader learnability vs training-data scale** — reused U's MLP-128 and V's causal GRU/TCN on fresh, pairwise sequence-disjoint i.i.d. ordinary-ADD pools over the *reduced* predeclared ladder **1500/4000/10000** (full ladder 1500/8000/32000; 200 full-batch updates at every scale); Class-A (E) best reader 0.107→0.116, best per-reader gain **+0.033** (sub-material); the flat MLP-128 anchor *declines* with data (L4 0.089→0.070); held-out ≤0.206; Bayes−reader gap 0.883 (≈unchanged from 0.892); training accuracy 0.13–0.23 (underfitting) → classification **W-B (data-saturated/bounded-limited)**: over 1500–10000 the V-B weak accessibility is not a simple training-data limitation and is consistent with a bounded-capacity/algorithmic limit; the fixed-budget vs bounded-capacity split is left open (`docs/reports/experiment_w_data_scale.md`).

**Superseded conclusions (kept explicit, per policy):** terminal-only supervision was the Phase-1 assumption until the generator-repair experiment; the combined objective F was expected to dominate E and did not; the mean-gate scalar played a larger role in Experiment G's framing than Experiment H's vector-level results support; the true-logit movement `⟨w_true, S_t−S_prev⟩` was the decision measure named in the Experiment H "next step" text, and Experiment I replaced it with the strongest-competitor decision margin (the true-logit measure is retained only as a secondary diagnostic). None of these earlier conclusions are hidden or rewritten — the dated records remain in `docs/reports/`.

## 4. Open questions

* **Experiment S localised R's open candidate-internal question and answered it negatively:** no internal candidate activation is more recoverable than `C_t`; the hard-trained chain weakly *increases* toward the output and held-out-operand generalization fails at every stage (S-C, computation-limited). The output-projection/geometry localisation is **not supported**; the remaining question is **upstream** of the candidate network (`experiment_s_candidate_internal.md`).
* Where **upstream of the candidate network** does the Class-A operand/modular information first become inaccessible — the observation encoder's inner features, the raw pre-transition state `S_{t-1}`, the gate pre/postactivation, or the pre-gated state `f_t⊙S_{t-1}`? Is the weakness a genuine information limit or a probe-family/label-organisation limit at the encoding stage? (`experiment_s_candidate_internal.md`.)
* **Experiment T answered the upstream question negatively (T-D).** The observation-encoder output `e_t` is structurally operand-blind (destination 1.000; `operand_a`/`operand_b` at chance); the state pathway is only descriptively stronger (+0.098, below the 0.10 bar) and its in-distribution gain **collapses on held-out operands**; the sigmoid does not lower accessibility; `cand_input` ≈ `h_reset` alone; no stage reaches the 0.50 bar or 0.25 held-out. The limitation is already present before/across the tested upstream representations (`experiment_t_upstream_representation.md`). **Remaining question:** is the operand information ever available at the **raw observation / task interface** (the single-step, register-indexed one-hot carries no operand values), or is the task interface itself the upstream constraint? Does a state-free **raw-observation history window** recover the target with held-out-operand generalisation?
* **Experiment U answered the raw-interface question (U-D).** The raw causal observation history **does** determine the operands/target: the current observation is operand-blind (L=1 target Bayes 0.290; operands 0.437/0.444), the **interface-identifiable horizon is L\*=4** (target 0.998, operands 0.999), and the **full causal prefix is uniquely sufficient** (Bayes 1.000); but the **bounded flat probe family stays weak** (best full-prefix 0.218 all-ADD, ~0.09 Class-A; held-out ≤0.25; gap 0.782). So T's operand-blindness is a property of the single-step observation/encoder, **not** of the task input. **Remaining question:** is the L4-identifiable information accessible to a **bounded sequence-aware reader** (small GRU / causal convolution with a fixed budget), or is the U-D gap a flat-representation artefact? If a bounded sequence-aware reader recovers the target, the question moves to the frozen recurrent state's retention; if not, the limitation is in bounded-reader access itself.
* **Experiment V answered the sequence-aware question negatively (V-B).** Two parameter-bounded causal readers (GRU hidden 85; TCN channels 52), each within the U MLP-128 budget, are also weak: Class-A 0.083 (GRU) / 0.112 (TCN) at L=4 and 0.080 / 0.109 at the full prefix, all-ADD never beating the flat baseline (0.214 vs 0.218), no reader ≥0.50, held-out ≤0.25 everywhere, **Bayes−reader gap 0.887**. So the U-D weak accessibility is not a flat-representation artefact, and temporal structure alone does not resolve it. **Remaining question:** is the persistent gap a **training-data / optimisation** limit of the bounded readers (they memorise) or a **bounded-capacity / algorithmic** limit — does recoverability rise toward the exact Bayes ceiling as the sequence-disjoint i.i.d. training pool grows, while training accuracy already rises?
* **Experiment W answered the data-scale question negatively over 1500–10000 (W-B).** Reusing the same predeclared readers on fresh, pairwise sequence-disjoint i.i.d. pools does not raise Class-A accuracy toward the ceiling: best reader 0.107→0.116, best gain **+0.033**, held-out ≤0.206, Bayes−reader gap 0.892→0.883; the readers underfit (training accuracy 0.13–0.23) and the flat MLP-128 anchor *declines* with data. So the persistent gap is **not** a simple training-data limitation in this range. **Remaining question:** is it the **fixed optimization protocol** (200 full-batch updates — the low training accuracy leaves this suspect) or a **bounded-capacity/algorithmic** limit — does raising the update budget at fixed data and fixed capacity recover held-out accuracy? (The predeclared 32k top of the ladder was not run.)
* Is the state↔class parity alignment a property of the committed-state placement or of the upstream encoder/candidate representation? Is the small readout parity geometry incidental or functional? (`experiment_n_parity_origin_localization.md`.)
* Is the third basin a systematic **modular/wrap** geometry (~55% of off-path failures are wrap) or an unsystematic off-axis collapse? Does the weaker off-path candidate margin partly drive the placement? (`experiment_m_representation_geometry.md`.)
* Does wrap-around admit any scalar-transfer fix, or is it a genuine representation/discretization limit? (Round-2 audit; H's candidate-wrong no-solution rate 78–82%.)
* Do repeated-ADD chains (terminal EM ≈ 0.15 under all objectives) require architecture-level changes?
* Can the ghost-free calibration of F be recovered inside the adopted E objective without a third loss term? (F report.)
* Long-horizon decay: T=20 ≈ 0.41 remains far below T=10 ≈ 0.76.
* Project-level questions retained from `docs/06` (continuous vs discrete search, 50+ step stability, recursion depth extrapolation, transactional deadlock) — all still open; some depend on phases not yet started.

## 6. Guardrails (current)

The full list lives in `docs/DOCUMENTATION_POLICY.md` §16. Current highlights: no novelty claims beyond the documented literature classification; no AGI/general-reasoning claims from Phase-1 results; probe decodability ≠ exact state preservation; oracle ≠ achievable; scalar gate summaries ≠ the vector-gated mechanism; a scalar-equivalent transfer statistic ≠ a correct (element-wise, off-path) commit; transition-level rescue ≠ sequence-level capability; an interventional commit-rule result ≠ the modified rule being the correct architecture or the representation being understood; a locally favorable delivery profile ≠ a sequence-level benefit; a frozen-readout localization of the off-path residual (latent placement vs readout bias) ≠ a causal claim or a fix, and "the fixed bias is rarely decisive" ≠ the readout being uninvolved; a measured modular/parity geometry ≠ an arithmetic-encoding claim or a mechanism that causes the failures, and "modularity" is not inferred from wrap being common (the wrap hypothesis is in fact rejected); a readout-*derived* parity axis ≠ the readout being the *origin* of the effect (the axis is built from W alone and tested against a value-label permutation null, and pairing-break controls are analysis-only counterfactuals, not interventions); permutation controls ≠ causal interventions; a local inference-time causal effect (O) ≠ a parity-specific mechanism or an architectural proof — a rescue that does not exceed a norm-matched control is generic perturbation, and a reference line for parity-based claims must be the matched control, not the baseline; a **first-error budget is an observed attribution, not a cause** — "candidate-origin sequence" means only that the earliest observed task-level error occurs when the frozen candidate is already wrong, and a numerically dominant class is not a validated recoverable target; **analysis-only probe recoverability ≠ the model "knows" the value, and a weak probe result ≠ proof the architecture cannot do modular arithmetic** — probe recoverability is measured under bounded analysis-only probes, and (Q-C) the target is only weakly recoverable from `C_t`, which is at most *consistent with* a candidate-representation limitation, never a causal claim;
